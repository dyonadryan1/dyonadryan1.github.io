Data Anda dan Google Drive

Data persediaan tersimpan di HP Anda. Gudang Offline tidak punya server, dan kami tidak pernah menerima data persediaan Anda.

Cadangan Google Drive bersifat pilihan. Saat dihubungkan, aplikasi hanya meminta akses ke berkas yang dibuatnya sendiri (izin drive.file), sehingga tidak bisa melihat isi Drive Anda yang lain, dan memakainya hanya untuk menyimpan serta memulihkan cadangan. Sambungan bisa diputus kapan saja di aplikasi, atau lewat myaccount.google.com/connections.

Penggunaan informasi yang diterima Gudang Offline dari Google API mematuhi Kebijakan Data Pengguna Layanan Google API, termasuk persyaratan Penggunaan Terbatas (Limited Use).

Gudang Offline

An inventory app for shops, warehouses and offices. Record stock in and out, scan barcodes and keep track of expiry dates — on your phone, without internet.

Install from Google Play Privacy policy

What you can do

  • Record stock without internetIn, out, damaged and lost, with a full history.
  • Scan barcodes and QR codesFind an item or record a movement with your phone camera.
  • Expiry remindersGet reminded before items expire, and see at a glance which items run low.
  • PDF and Excel reportsStock movement, handover and loss reports with your own letterhead; Excel import and export; QR labels.
  • Backup to your own Google DriveAutomatically every day, or to a file to move to a new phone.
  • App lockWith your fingerprint or screen lock.

Your data and Google Drive

Your inventory is stored on your phone. Gudang Offline has no server, and we never receive your inventory data.

Google Drive backup is optional. When you connect it, the app asks only for access to the files it creates itself (the drive.file permission), so it cannot see the rest of your Drive, and it uses that access only to save and restore your backup. You can disconnect in the app at any time, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Gudang Offline’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Details are in the privacy policy.
